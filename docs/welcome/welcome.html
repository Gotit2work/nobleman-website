Live Production

Your program is premium. Your production should match.

“Anything that can go wrong on a livestream will, in front of everyone.”

So we build in backups and fallback plans before show day, and record a backup of the audio.

  • Several camera angles mixed live, with branded graphics

  • A technical director running the show
