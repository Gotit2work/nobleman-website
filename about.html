About Nobleman

A film crew shaped by Apple, Hollywood, the Emmys, and NASA.

Nobleman Productions is a premium video and content strategy team operating from San Diego and Las Vegas, building brands that look credible on camera and convert viewers into clients.

Our story

Beautiful video alone doesn’t grow an audience. Strategy does.

Most production companies are great with a camera. We are too — but we noticed a gap. Beautiful video alone doesn’t grow an audience or book new clients. Strategy does. So we built a company that brings both to every engagement.

Our founders, Justin Edelman and Jean Gotay, met at Platt College San Diego. Justin spent years inside Apple as a video engineer, worked specialty camera on major Hollywood productions, served as a camera technician on Emmy-recognized productions, and supported a NASA project. Jean, an Army veteran, built a career in real estate, music videos, and live streaming, bringing the creative fire and energy that keeps every project moving forward.

Today, Nobleman Productions serves brands across San Diego, Las Vegas, and beyond — running YouTube growth retainers, broadcasting live launches for course creators, and capturing conferences and corporate events. The standard is the same on every engagement: Hollywood quality, strategy that compounds.

Founders

Justin Edelman & Jean Gotay

Together, they lead a veteran-owned production company that pairs Hollywood-level execution with the strategy required to turn content into real growth.
