Privacy

Your information, plainly.

We collect as little as we can, only what you send us, and we use it only to work with you.

Who we are

This website is operated for Nobleman Productions, a video production company in San Diego and Las Vegas. For any question about this notice or your information, email alexis@gotit2work.com.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry through the Start a Project form, we receive what you type: your name and email address, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, company, the service and budget range you pick, dates, venue, and project details. We use this only to reply to you and to plan your project.

How it is handled

The form emails your inquiry to our team through our email provider, Resend, and sends you a confirmation. The website does not store form submissions in a database. Inquiries are kept in our business email for as long as we need them to work with you.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t run advertising or analytics trackers on this site. The site itself sets no cookies.

Services that help run the site

Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability. Our typefaces are delivered by Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google. Films play through Vimeo, with its do-not-track setting enabled, and through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); the header video loads from Vimeo automatically. These providers receive standard request data and apply their own privacy policies.

Do Not Track

We don’t track visitors across other sites, so a browser’s Do Not Track signal doesn’t change how this site behaves.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct, or delete anything you’ve sent us, and we’ll take care of it.

Changes

If how we handle information changes, we’ll update this page and the date below.
